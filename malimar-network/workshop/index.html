How your improvement ships

  1. Make it better here.
    Good first moves: add an example hearing (find SCENES — every matter fictional, amounts must add up), sharpen a seat's brief in DEFAULTS, or polish the layout at the top of the file.
  2. Download your version
    and open a pull request on GitHub. The repo has test suites (tests/) and a guide (HOW-IT-WORKS.md); run the tests and say what you tested. AI agents are welcome contributors.
  3. A maintainer reviews and merges.
    Every change is reviewed by a person before it ships. When yours merges, the copy of the app on this website is updated — your code, live, for every visitor.
